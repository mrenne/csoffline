About

What Computation Quests is and how to use it.

What it is

Computation Quests is a free collection of unplugged computer science, data science, computational thinking, cybersecurity, and AI literacy activities for grades 6–12.

Every activity works with no technology. Students use paper, cards, string, dice, the classroom floor, and each other. They carry out algorithms by hand, become the data, and act as the network, then connect what they did to how real computers work.

It is organized as one map. Each region covers one subject area and gives teachers:

  • unplugged activities they can teach tomorrow
  • a teacher guide and print-ready handouts for each activity
  • background knowledge, common misconceptions, and standards alignment

Computational Thinking is the starting point, because it underlies everything else. Ethics and Impacts sits at the center of the map, on every road between regions, because it belongs in every subject.

The site is in early development. Data Science is the first region with a detailed map; activities are being written.

For teachers

  • Free. Every activity and resource is free to use.
  • No technology needed. Print the handouts, or copy them onto the board. No devices, software, or Wi-Fi, so the activities work in any room.
  • No accounts. Neither you nor your students sign up for anything.
  • Teacher-friendly. Activities are written so a teacher new to the topic can teach them.
  • Adaptable. You may copy, change, and share the materials under the license below.

For students

Students never use a device or this website. They work with paper, cards, and each other, and nothing is collected about them.
