# Cybersecurity

> A region of Computation Quests. Status: **not started**. Every activity is unplugged:
> students use no technology.

## Overview

Students learn how systems and information are attacked and defended, from passwords and phishing to encryption.

## Topics (draft)

- Passwords and authentication
- Social engineering
- Encryption
- Privacy
- Networks and the internet

## Activity ideas

Unplugged ideas, one or more per topic, to write up as activities.

| Topic | Idea | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| Passwords and authentication | **Dice Passphrases** | Students roll dice to pick words from a printed list, then count how many guesses an attacker would need to find each passphrase. |
| Social engineering | **Phish or Legit?** | Students sort printed messages into real and fake, name the clues, and role-play a pretexting phone call. |
| Encryption | **Cipher Wheels** | Students build paper cipher wheels, exchange secret messages, then try to crack each other's messages by counting letter frequencies. |
| Privacy | **Footprint Trail** | From a trail of clue cards (a photo caption, a check-in, a team roster), students work out how much a stranger could learn about a fictional student. |
| Networks and the internet | **Human Internet** | Students act as computers and routers, passing addressed paper packets across the room and handling lost and out-of-order packets. |
