# AI Literacy

> A region of Computation Quests. Status: **not started**. Every activity is unplugged:
> students use no technology.

## Overview

Students learn how AI systems learn from examples, where they succeed and fail, and how to evaluate what they produce.

## Topics (draft)

- How machine learning works
- Training data
- Generative AI
- Evaluating AI output
- Bias in AI systems

## Activity ideas

Unplugged ideas, one or more per topic, to write up as activities.

| Topic | Idea | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| How machine learning works | **Paper Classifier** | Groups sort picture cards into two groups, write rules that explain the sort, then test their rules on new cards. |
| Training data | **Different Decks** | Each group trains its paper classifier on a different card deck, and the class compares how the classifiers disagree on the same new cards. |
| Generative AI | **Next-Word Dice** | Students tally which word follows which in a short text, then generate new sentences by rolling dice against their tally table, like a tiny language model. |
| Evaluating AI output | **Fact Check** | Students check printed, AI-style answers against source cards and find the confident mistakes. |
| Bias in AI systems | **Who Gets Missed?** | Students look for whose faces, voices, or names are missing from a training deck, then predict who the classifier will get wrong. |
