## Unplugged collections

### CS Unplugged

- **Link:** https://www.csunplugged.org/
- **Type:** unplugged activity collection
- **Grade band:** 6-8 (many activities also suit younger students)
- **Big ideas:** data representation, algorithms, and more across computer science
- **Author / source:** CS Education Research Group, University of Canterbury
- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (the same as ours, so activities can be adapted with credit)
- **Unplugged?** yes
- **Why it's useful:** The best-known collection of no-device CS activities, and a model for the
  format.
