Big ideas

  1. Data is made by people for a purpose. Every dataset reflects choices about what to collect, how, and from whom.
  2. Data must be organized and cleaned. Structure (rows, columns, types) and cleaning come before analysis.
  3. Visualizations tell stories, and can mislead. Choosing and reading charts critically.
  4. Patterns and models help us predict, with uncertainty. From trends to simple models to the ideas behind machine learning.
  5. Data has consequences. Privacy, bias, and fairness in how data is collected and used.

These follow the data investigation cycle: ask a question → collect or consider data → analyze → interpret and communicate.
