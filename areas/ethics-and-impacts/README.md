# Ethics and Impacts

> A region of Computation Quests. Status: **not started**. Every activity is unplugged:
> students use no technology.

## Overview

At the center of the map, where every road meets. Students examine how computing affects individuals, communities, and society.

## Topics (draft)

- Bias and fairness
- Privacy and surveillance
- Accessibility
- Computing and society
- Responsible design

## Activity ideas

Unplugged ideas, one or more per topic, to write up as activities.

| Topic | Idea | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| Bias and fairness | **The Fair Rule** | Groups write a rule for choosing who gets a limited prize, test it on a deck of applicant cards, and find who the rule leaves out. |
| Privacy and surveillance | **Where Do You Stand?** | Students stand along a line from agree to disagree on data scenarios, explain their positions, and look for what would change their minds. |
| Accessibility | **Design for Everyone** | Using user persona cards, groups redesign a paper app screen so each persona can use it. |
| Computing and society | **Futures Wheel** | Starting from a new technology, students draw rings of first, second, and third effects on chart paper. |
| Responsible design | **Town Hall** | Students take stakeholder role cards and debate whether their town should adopt an algorithm, such as one that schedules buses. |
