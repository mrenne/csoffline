Computation Quests

Each region of the map covers one area of computer science, with unplugged activities that need only paper, cards, and the people in the room. Begin in Computational Thinking in the south, then follow the roads. They all meet in Ethics and Impacts at the center.

Everything is free, and students never need a device.
