## What's really going on

Computers can seem fair because they follow rules exactly, but those rules and the data behind them come from people. If the data leaves some groups out, or reflects unfair choices from the past, a system can repeat those patterns at a huge scale. That's how a face scanner can work well for some people and poorly for others. Finding bias means asking who made the data, who's missing from it, and who could be harmed by the results.
