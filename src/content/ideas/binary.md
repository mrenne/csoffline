## What's really going on

Inside a computer, everything is stored as switches that are either off or on, written as 0 and 1. A single 0 or 1 is called a bit. Put enough bits together and they can stand for any number, and with an agreed-on code, those numbers can stand for letters, colors in a photo, or sounds in a song. Every text, picture, and video on your phone is a very long string of 0s and 1s.
