## What's really going on

A table with thousands of numbers is hard to read, but a chart can show a pattern at a glance. Data visualization means turning data into pictures like bar graphs, line graphs, and maps so people can spot trends, compare groups, and notice things that don't fit. The choices you make, like which chart to use and where the scale starts, can make the same data tell a clear story or a misleading one.
