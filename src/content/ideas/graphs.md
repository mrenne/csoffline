## What's really going on

In computer science, a graph is a set of dots, called nodes, joined by lines, called edges. Nodes can stand for anything, like places, people, or web pages, and edges show how they connect. Map apps use graphs to find the fastest route, and social networks use them to suggest friends. Turning a problem into a graph often makes it much easier to solve.
