## Overview

This lesson sequence uses unplugged activities and manipulatives to teach data science. It ends with
students choosing an original research question based on their own interests and answering it with
the skills they have built.

## What students learn

- Collect data with techniques such as surveys, public data sets, and crowdsourcing.
- Interpret, create, and evaluate data visualizations, and explain trends to non-technical audiences.
- Find patterns and relationships with descriptive statistics, visualizations, and modeling.
- Make predictions with models, including linear regression.
- Evaluate models and spot what makes them unreliable, such as bias and overfitting.
- Examine the role of data science in society, including misleading visualizations and biased data
  collection.
