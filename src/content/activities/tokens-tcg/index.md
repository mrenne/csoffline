## What you'll do

Build a deck of real AI concepts, then face another player. Deploy AI Systems, run Tasks to earn
Tokens, and Audit your opponent's Systems. Every card explains its idea in plain language and asks
a question to talk about.

## How it works

Build a deck of at least 25 cards from one or two colors, with no more than two copies of any card.
There are three kinds of card:

- **Systems** score points and fight.
- **Tools** stay out and can be used again each turn.
- **Actions** happen once, then are discarded.

On your turn, turn your cards upright, draw a card, add one card to your Training Set for Data, and
play cards. Your Systems can then Run a Task, which scores Tokens but turns them sideways, or Run an
Audit against a sideways System. When a System has as many Flags as its Trust, it is Deprecated.

## Winning

The first player to 15 Tokens wins.

## Afterward

- Which card's Discuss question changed how you think about AI?
- Which ideas from the game have you seen in AI tools you know?
