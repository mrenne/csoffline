## Overview

Tokens TCG is a physical trading card game for teaching AI literacy in grades 6–8 without students
using a device. The Alpha Set has 70 cards. Each card has a plain-language definition and a question
to discuss.

## Getting the cards

[Download the print-and-play PDF](https://tokenstcg.com/design/print/tokens-print-and-play.pdf)
(8 pages, 9 cards per US Letter page, with cut marks). Print at Actual Size / 100%. One print is one
full set of 70 cards. It's free to print, with no sign-up.

Every student gets the same base set of Common, Uncommon, and Rare cards. The two Chase cards are
separate, and you decide how students earn one, such as through a class challenge or a raffle.

Card sleeves are optional. Backing each printed card with an old trading card makes it easier to
shuffle.

## Ways to run it

- **Quick Play (10–20 minutes, 2 players):** the full game. Demo a few turns first.
- **Discussion Mode: Draw the Line (20–30 minutes, whole class):** a good first lesson before
  anyone learns the rules.
- **Launch Day (25–30 minutes, co-op):** students work together on the AI alignment problem.

The [Educator Resources page](https://tokenstcg.com/educator_resources/) has rules sheets, player
reference cards, the Draw the Line teacher guide, debrief questions, and a vocabulary guide. Students
can learn the game from [How to Play](https://tokenstcg.com/how_to_play/) and browse every card in
the [Alpha Set](https://tokenstcg.com/alpha_set/).

## What students learn

The five card colors match the five categories of the CSTA/AI4K12 AI Learning Priorities for All
K-12 Students, and most cards trace to a row in its Grades 6–8 tables:

- **Green:** Humans and AI
- **Blue:** Representation & Reasoning
- **Black:** Machine Learning
- **White:** Ethical AI Design
- **Red:** Societal Impacts of AI

Stats in the Alpha Set are still being tuned through playtesting, so costs and numbers may change.
