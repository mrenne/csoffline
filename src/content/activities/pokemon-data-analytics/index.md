## What you'll do

You're a member of a Student Analytics Team investigating Pokémon TCG deck performance. Your
guiding question: which deck characteristics and card variables contribute most to match success?

## Your team

- **Lead Analyst:** oversees the guiding question, keeps the pace, and pulls together the overall trends.
- **Data Recorder:** logs all match variables accurately during play.
- **Quality Assurance Specialist:** checks for entry errors and makes sure the data is consistent.
- **Visualization & Strategy Presenter:** summarizes the findings and leads the final strategy pitch.

## How it works

1. Pair up decks and play 3–4 short, structured matches. The Data Recorder logs every variable, and
   the Quality Assurance Specialist reviews each row after each match.
2. Clean your table, then compare winning decks against losing decks.
3. Pick one clear trend in your data and explain whether it proves causation or shows only correlation.
4. Write a 3-sentence deck recommendation for a client that cites at least two quantitative metrics
   from your data.

## Afterward

- How did standardized data collection rules affect the reliability and consistency of your team's conclusions?
- Which gameplay variable proved most significant in predicting match success, and why?
- How do professional data analysts or game developers use similar quantitative methods to balance
  competitive games and improve user experience?
