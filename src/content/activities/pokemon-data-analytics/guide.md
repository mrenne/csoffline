## Overview

Students work as an analytics team investigating which deck characteristics and card variables
contribute most to match success in the Pokémon Trading Card Game.

## Learning objectives

- Identify measurable gameplay variables.
- Collect and organize standardized match data.
- Calculate basic descriptive statistics.
- Formulate an evidence-based deck recommendation.

## Pacing

| Part | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Team roles and variable setup | 10 minutes |
| Structured gameplay and data collection | 20 minutes |
| Data analysis and strategy recommendation | 15 minutes |
| Exit ticket and final reflection | 5 minutes |

If playing live matches isn't practical, teams can simulate matches or review recorded match logs.

## Variables to record

| Variable | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Match ID and Winner | Unique match identifier and winning deck label (for example, Match 1, Deck A vs. Deck B, Winner: Deck A) |
| Primary Pokémon Type | Dominant energy or type of the active strategy (for example, Fire, Water, Grass, Lightning) |
| Average HP of Active Pokémon | Mean Hit Points of Pokémon brought into the active spot during the match |
| Average Energy Cost per Attack | Mean number of Energy cards required to execute attacks |
| Trainer Cards Played | Total Item, Supporter, and Stadium cards played by the winning deck |
| Total Turns to Win | Total full turns completed until the victory condition was met |
