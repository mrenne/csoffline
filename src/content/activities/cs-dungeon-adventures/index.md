## What you'll do

You're a brand-new adventurer working your way down through a dungeon, five floors deep. On every
floor, collect DATA, avoid the bugs that drain your RAM, and reach the RUN square to go down to the
next floor.

## How it works

Each turn, roll the die. An odd roll moves you diagonally and an even roll moves you straight. Draw
your path as you go. Everything you cross happens to you, in order: bugs, data, power-ups, and the
floor's own puzzle, such as a program to run or a code to crack. Keep track of your RAM and DATA in
the boxes at the bottom of each page.

After every floor, swap booklets with a partner for a code review. Check each other's path, math,
and puzzle, and fix any mistakes together.

## Winning

Get through all five floors. If your RAM runs out, your adventurer crashes. Tally the crash, then
start the next floor fresh and keep playing.

## Afterward

- What makes a program predictable?
- Did the order you did things in change the result? Why?
- When an AI sorts things unfairly, who is responsible: the AI, its training data, or the people who made it?
