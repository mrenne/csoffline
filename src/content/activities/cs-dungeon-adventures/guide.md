## Overview

Computer Science Dungeon Adventures is a series of unplugged, paper-based games for grades 6–10.
Students play alone with a printed booklet, one six-sided die, and a pencil. Each of the five floors
in a booklet teaches an idea from a different area: algorithms and design, programming, data and
analysis, systems and security, and computing and society, with AI woven throughout. About 10 games
are planned.

## The booklets

Each booklet is 9 pages, US Letter, black and white: a cover and tracker, how to play, five floors, a
shop and ranks page, and a teacher answer key.

- [Dungeon 1: The Web (PDF)](https://github.com/mrenne/cs-dungeon-adventures/blob/main/games/01_web/Dungeon_1_The_Web_Booklet.pdf)
- [Dungeon 2: The Smartphone (PDF)](https://github.com/mrenne/cs-dungeon-adventures/blob/main/games/02_smartphone/Dungeon_2_The_Smartphone_Booklet.pdf)

Both booklets are built but have not been tested in a classroom yet.

## Running it

Students check each other's work in code review pairs after every floor, instead of the teacher
marking it. Keep the last page of each booklet, the teacher answer key, for settling disputes. It
also has discussion starters for each floor.

Standards codes have not been mapped yet.
