## What you'll do

You're a hiring manager. A client needs a data science team for a real project, like wildfire
alerts, outbreak tracking, or an esports league. You have a budget and three spots to fill. The
catch: while you draft, you can see only the front of each card, with the job title, salary tier,
and tech skills. The real salaries, degrees, and job duties are on the back.

## How it works

1. **Setup.** Form 2 or 3 teams. Lay the 12 career cards face-up, draw a Project Quest card, and
   draw a Market Event card that changes the game for everyone. Each team secretly draws a Company
   card.
2. **Blind draft.** Take turns picking career cards in snake order (A, B, C, C, B, A, and so on)
   until every team has three.
3. **Audit.** Flip your cards and score your team: points for each required role, for staying on
   budget, and for tech skills your team members share.
4. **Fast trade.** Starting with the lowest score, each team makes one trade with the leftover cards.
   Icons on the backs of the cards give special trade abilities.
5. **Re-audit and pitch.** Score your team again. Then give a 30-second pitch explaining why your
   team fits the project.

## Winning

Your final score is your audit score, plus your secret Company bonus, plus your pitch score. If
there's a tie, the team whose lineup needs less schooling wins.

## Afterward

- Which job surprised you most when you flipped it?
- Was the most expensive role worth it?
- What schooling would your dream job need?
